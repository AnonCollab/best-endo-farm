# Best Endo Farm — Warframe

A single-page guide to the best Endo farms in Warframe, with every farm cross-checked against Digital Extremes' official patch notes through **17 October 2026**.

**Live site:** https://anoncollab.github.io/best-endo-farm/

## The verdict

Nothing has dethroned the old meta, and nothing important was nerfed — every classic farm still works. The one big change went the other way: **Arbitrations were buffed in Update 41.1 (11 Feb 2026)**.

- **Best premade squad:** Steel Path Rathuum Arena (Yam/Vodyanoi, Sedna) — Nidus + Khora (Pilfering Strangledome) + 2× Nekros, one Mod Drop Chance Booster in the squad. ~2,000–4,000 Endo/run claimed; no patch touched it in 2024–2026.
- **Best solo / all-round:** Arbitrations (Defense) — 11,796 Endo/hr in the wiki's worked example; buffed by U41.1, unlocked earlier by U39, faster Defense rotations since U38.5.
- **Best short sessions:** Höllvania Tier 5 and Nightcap Deepmine Steel Path bounties (official tables: up to 4,200 Endo per roll).
- **Weekly stack:** Palladino's 6,000 Endo for **10 Riven Slivers** (weekly, since U42), Teshin's rotating **30,000 Endo for 150 Steel Essence**, Maroo's weekly hunt, Sorties, and dissolving duplicate mods.

The page includes the ranked shortlist, a grind-time calculator, per-farm methods and requirements, the full official bounty tables, a per-farm patch-note cross-check table, outdated guidance to ignore, and sources. Rates are labelled claimed vs official throughout — no source publishes a controlled Endo/hour measurement.
