The farms in detail

1. Rathuum Arena — Sedna (Yam / Vodyanoi) best premade

Method. Executioners drop Endo directly (15/50/80 Endo from their mod drop table) plus mods you dissolve afterwards; the mission reward table also includes Endo ×250. Nidus (Larva) pulls Executioners into one spot, Khora's Pilfering Strangledome holds and loots them, two Nekros Desecrate the corpses. Kills must be Slash-heavy (weapon with >50% of its physical damage as Slash) so bodies dismember into extra Desecrate-able parts. Per the community Endo Farming Guide, Vodyanoi gives no more Endo than Yam — it's just harder (SP Vodyanoi is level 185). Yam is the pick for newer farmers. Entry: Yam 10, Vodyanoi 15 Judgement Points.

Booster myth-bust: Endo drops from enemies count as mod drops. The Mod Drop Chance Booster is the big multiplier and stacks with Steel Path's built-in +100% mod drop chance; only one squad member needs it. The Resource Drop Chance Booster does NOT apply — any guide crediting it for Arena is wrong.

Rates (all claimed). Optimised premade: "possible to get 4,000 Endo in one mission" (Endo Discord guide); 2,000–4,000 per run (2026 farms guide); ~4,000 in a ~1-minute run at peak with boosters (2026 video). Solo Khora: ~1,000 per ~2-minute run (2026 video). Hourly claims for a good squad: ~10,000–20,000, with short-run peaks far above that once loading and Judgement-Point upkeep are ignored.

Verdict: still works — not patched. No 2024–2026 patch touched Rathuum spawns, Executioner drop tables or Endo drops, and 2026 guides still rank it #1. One caveat: guides claiming Desecrate and Pilfering Strangledome "double-dip" the same corpse are outdated — DE ended that in Update 25.0 (May 2019); a dead target yields one successful loot roll.

2. Arbitrations best solo

Method. Endless elite missions on an AABBC… rotation: Rotation A = 900 Endo, B = 1,200, C = 1,500, plus Ayatan sculptures as rewards and Vitus Essence from Arbitration Shield Drones. Defense is the popular pick because rotations now complete every 3 waves, a reward roughly every ~3 minutes with a fast squad or a solo AoE frame (e.g. Mesa).

Rates. 11,796 Endo/hour (wiki worked example, including filled Ayatan sculptures, assuming one reward per 5 minutes); ~10,000–15,000/hr claimed by guides. A creator re-tested the farm right after the Feb 2026 buff and confirmed the improved Rotation C rates.

Vitus correction. Vitus Essence is not directly convertible to Endo — there is no "buy Endo with Vitus" item. The link is indirect: the Honors store sells Ayatan sculptures and the reusable Ayatan Amber Star blueprint, and filled sculptures trade to Maroo for Endo. Resource Boosters do double Vitus pickups; loot abilities do not affect the drones.

Requirements. No Steel Path. Since Update 39 (25 Jun 2025) Arbitrations unlock with a task in the Pluto → Eris Junction — any guide saying "complete every Star Chart node" is outdated. Solo-viable, no boosters required.

Verdict: still works — and BUFFED. Update 41.1 (11 Feb 2026): Rotation C Endo ×1,500 went 33% → 35% and 3× Vitus Essence 7% → 10%. Update 38.5 (19 Mar 2025) cut Defense rotations from 5 waves to 3. The only historical nerf is from 2019 and is already baked into every modern guide.

4. Railjack, Ayatan sculptures, dissolving mods passive

  • Railjack: Grineer Skirmish in Veil Proxima — 1,200-Endo caches and scrapping salvaged wreckage pays Endo directly (since Update 29.10 replaced Dirac 1:1 with Endo in 2021). ~6,000–10,000/hr claimed. No 2025–2026 change. Mid-tier rate, high side value (credits, relics via Void Storms, mods to dissolve).
  • Ayatan sculptures (filled values, wiki): Anasa 3,450 · Kitha 3,000 · Orta 2,700 · Zambuka/Hemakara 2,600 · Vaya 1,800 · Piv 1,725 · Valana 1,575 · Sah 1,500 · Ayr 1,425. Loose stars: Cyan 50, Amber 100. No patch has altered these values.
  • Maroo's weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt (Stolen Dreams; resets Monday 00:00 UTC): one guaranteed sculpture per week. Small but free.
  • Sorties / Archon Hunts feed the pipeline: Anasa sculpture @ 28.00% and 4,000 Endo @ 12.00% in the Sortie table — and Sorties are a source of the 3-day Mod Drop Chance Booster (3.27%) that powers the Arena farm.
  • Dissolving duplicate mods: Common 5 · Uncommon 10 · Rare 15 · Primed 20 · Requiem 1,000 (500 if defiled) · Rivens 115 + 200 × rerolls. The universal passive source and the tail-end of every drop farm. Dissolve values unchanged.

5. The weekly stack (2025–2026 additions) weekly

  • Palladino (Iron Wake) — added Update 42 (25 Mar 2026): a 6,000 Endo purchase, weekly limit 1, paid in Riven Slivers. Price: 10 Riven Slivers (wiki, Palladino page — Wares; verified 17 Oct 2026).
  • Teshin Steel Path Honors — 30,000 Endo is one of 8 weekly rotating offers, buyable once in its rotation week only. Price: 150 Steel Essence (wiki, Steel Essence page — rotating offers grid; all 8 offers total 730 Steel Essence, which cross-checks; verified 17 Oct 2026).
  • Descendia (Update 41, Dec 2025) — weekly tower; Normal-path common rewards randomly include 2,500 Endo (one weekly run reported ~10,000 total). Steel Path commons are poor for direct Endo. A weekly bonus, not a farm.
  • The Perita Rebellion (U41) — Rotation A has 2,500 Endo at only 5%. Not a farm.
  • Epoch Operation: Taubound (event, 15 Oct – 17 Nov 2026) — its shop's only Endo item is a trivial 100 Endo (limit 300). Not an Endo farm despite being the newest content.

Outdated guidance to ignore

  • Pre-2019 guides implying Desecrate + Pilfering Strangledome double-dip one corpse (fixed in U25.0).
  • Any guide listing the Resource Drop Chance Booster as helping Arena Endo — it's mod-drop chance only.
  • Guides saying Arbitrations require a fully completed Star Chart (changed U39, Jun 2025), or quoting pre-U41.1 Rotation C rates (33% / 7%).
  • TheGamer's "1,000 Endo per minute" Arena figure (~2022) — internally inconsistent marketing copy.
